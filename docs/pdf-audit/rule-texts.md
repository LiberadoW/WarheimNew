# PDF audit: special-rule texts (group rule-texts)

Source: rulebook PDF AD2026.04.15. Scope: the texts shown for unit rules (UI and the optional PDF rules appendix), looked up through `getRuleText`.

## What was built

- `src/Data.js/PdfRules.js` (new, generated by a script kept in the scratchpad): 453 rule texts.
  - Chapter 8 (Zasady specjalne, s. 34-59): every rule heading. Sub-sections were folded into their parent rule as `HEADING:` paragraphs: level-3/4 headings (BARWY WOJENNE colours, GNOBLAR-… under OZNAKOWANY GNOBLAR, PIĘTNO … under PIĘTNO CHAOSU, PRZYMIOT options, SPACZ… under TECHNOMANTA, LEKTYKA/PALANKIN/ISTOTA ETERYCZNA sub-sections, ZNAK CHAOSU groups, …), the monster boxes printed away from their rule (Krwiopuszcz → DEMONOLOG, Furia Slaanesha → RYTUAŁ BŁOGIEJ MĘKI, Niebieski strachulec → STRACHULEC, ŁADUNEK WYBUCHOWY… → ŻYWA BOMBA), and the bottom-of-page tables under the rule that references them (e.g. TABELA DARY CHAOSU → OKO CHAOSU, TABELA AWARII PLUGAWEJ TECHNOLOGII! → TECHNOMANTA, TABELA NIEWYPAŁU! BRONI PROCHOWEJ → ZAWODNY).
  - Chapter 7 (Psychologia, s. 30-33): ANIMOZJA, BERSERK, FURIA, GROZA, GŁÓD KRWI, GŁUPOTA, NIENAWIŚĆ, NIEZŁOMNY, ODWIECZNY KONFLIKT, SAM W WALCE, STRACH (with their sub-sections and tables) replace the chapter 8 "Zobacz ROZDZIAŁ 7" / "Więcej informacji znajduje się w ROZDZIALE 7" cross-references.
  - Chapter 21 (Stygmaty Chaosu, s. 543-549): one entry per stigma rule used by a unit, combining the individual stigma texts: Stygmat Khorna (kolczasty ogon), Stygmaty Nurgla (odór), Stygmaty Nurgla (rój much), Stygmaty Khorna (skoczek), Stygmaty Nurgla (odór, rój much), Stygmaty Slaanesha (ogon skorpiona, ponętny, szczypce).
  - Rules defined outside chapter 8: Konny (all band-page variants, s. 65 and 117), Towarzysz Graala and Ślubowanie (s. 117), Mistrzowie koni (s. 242), Żelazny Demon (s. 207), Magia krwi with its Uroki (s. 253), Ambona myśliwska (s. 143), Reflektant (s. 177), Gnoblar-pochodnia (Miotacz ołowiu, s. 490), Duża dźgacz (ch. 14, s. 428), Magiczna ochrona (ch. 6, s. 27), Parowóz (the whole PAROWÓZ section of ch. 18, s. 528-529), and Strachulce (alias of STRACHULEC, see below).
  - Extraction: lines come from the default pdftotext output (reading order) and were re-spaced from the `-layout` output of the same page, so glued words such as "zasadzispecjalnej" are gone (a test asserts no token of 30+ characters remains; a dictionary check over the whole set found no glued words left). Rule names use normal casing; parameters such as (X) or (1-3) are dropped because `getRuleText` ignores them anyway.
- `src/Functions/getRuleText.js`: precedence is now pdfRules > siteRules > rulesObject. Normalisation is unchanged except that en/em dashes are folded to "-" ("Hop–siup" = "Hop-siup"). PdfRules keys with a parameter (the stigma combinations) are matched on the full name first, so "Stygmaty Nurgla (odór)" and "Stygmaty Nurgla (odór, rój much)" get different texts.
- `src/Functions/getRuleText.test.js`: "almost every unit rule has a text" (<10% missing) became "every unit rule in every band has a text", now also covering mercenaries. The whitelist of rules without a PDF text is **empty**: every unit rule resolves to a PDF text. New tests cover PDF precedence (Męczennik, Żywy święty, Za mną!), folded sub-sections, stigma combinations, the rules from outside chapter 8, and glued words.

Rules the band agents added during this audit, all with PDF texts: Męczennik (s. 44), Żywy święty (s. 59), Strachliwy (s. 51), Za mną! (s. 57), Pawężnik (s. 47), Oprych (s. 46), Magia krwi (s. 253).

## Notes

- Some PDF typos are kept verbatim because the PDF is the source of truth ("specjalenj", "spółczynnika", "podlegajacy", "KSIĘGA GROBOWCÓW", "złożowe").
- Long sections carry their tables as plain text rows (e.g. "1-2 OJ TY…!"), not formatted tables.
- The generator lives in the scratchpad (`ruletexts/gen.js`, `emit.js`); re-running it regenerates PdfRules.js.
